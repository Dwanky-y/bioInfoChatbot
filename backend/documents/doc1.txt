Toy Name: Minh's Robot

Description: Minh's Robot is an interactive toy designed to engage children in learning basic programming and robotics. It features a variety of sensors and motors, allowing it to move, detect obstacles, and respond to voice commands.

Troubleshooting Problems:
1. Problem: The robot does not turn on.
    Solution: Ensure the batteries are properly installed and fully charged. Check the power switch and try resetting the robot.

2. Problem: The robot is not responding to voice commands.
    Solution: Make sure the microphone is not obstructed and that you are speaking clearly. Verify that the robot is in listening mode.

3. Problem: The robot is not moving correctly.
    Solution: Check for any obstacles blocking the robot's path. Ensure the wheels and motors are free of debris and functioning properly.

History: This toy once exploded in a children's hospital causing the toy to be recalled and destroyed. The recall was on 1/15/2025
